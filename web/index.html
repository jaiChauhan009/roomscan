    How to capture (1 minute read)

    Before you start: open every interior door, switch on all lights, wipe the lens, keep the zoom at 1x, hold the phone upright at chest height.

    Photos (2 to 8 per room)

    1. Stand in the doorway you came in by, back against the door frame.
    2. Take 5–6 photos turning from left to right, each overlapping the last by about a third. Floor and ceiling in every photo.
    3. Every room except the first: turn around and take one photo looking back into the room you came from.

    Send originals (Files, AirDrop, cable). Chat apps shrink photos and they will be rejected.

    Video (about a minute per room)

    1. Video mode, 1x. Walk slowly, one step every two seconds, around the room.
    2. Tilt up to the ceiling once in each room. Keep some floor in view; never turn on the spot.

    LiDAR (Pro iPhone / iPad Pro, 30–40 s per room)

    1. Record with the free Stray Scanner app: walk slowly round each room, tilt up to the ceiling and down to the floor once.
    2. Files app > On My iPhone > Stray Scanner: press and hold the newest folder > Compress, then add the .zip here.

    Optional: printed A4 scale marker

    For photos and video, tape one printed marker (printed at 100 %, square 180 mm) flat on a wall at chest height in each room, and keep it in view in 3 or more photos or a few seconds of video. It makes the sizes much more accurate.

    Sizes (L / B / H)

    Optional, in metres. One is enough. For photos and video, a length or breadth (wall to wall) improves the scale; a ceiling height alone is only compared with ours, not used for scale.
